Passport & Visa

Your Nigerian passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your travel date. Your tourist visa is included and coordinated by Wakapadi.

Health

Yellow Fever vaccination is required if travelling from a Yellow Fever country. Malaria prophylaxis is strongly recommended — consult your doctor four to six weeks before departure. Ensure routine vaccinations are current.

Weather

August falls in Zanzibar's dry season — the finest time to visit. Expect warm, sunny conditions between 28–32°C with cooling ocean breezes.

Currency

The Tanzanian Shilling is the local currency. USD is widely accepted. Inform your Nigerian bank of your travel dates before departure.

What to Pack

Light linens, cotton resort wear and swimwear. Smart casual for dinners. A modest wrap or shawl for Stone Town and cultural sites. Comfortable walking shoes. High-factor sunscreen — the Zanzibari sun is generous.

Cultural Etiquette

Zanzibar is a predominantly Muslim island. Please dress modestly in public areas away from the beach. Greet locals warmly — "Jambo!" is always welcome. Alcohol availability is limited in many areas; Wakapadi recommends embracing the island's culture fully and with respect.
